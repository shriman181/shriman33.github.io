How it works

From raw field data
to actionable intelligence.

1
Ingest
Satellite imagery, IoT sensors, Agmarknet mandi prices, weather APIs, and ERP records stream into the Stellrix pipeline — no migration required.
2
Model
AI models fuse multi-source signals at field-parcel granularity. Every new farm calibrates the models further — a data moat that compounds with scale.
3
Prescribe
Yield forecasts, input recommendations, and traceability records surface as dashboard insights, API outputs, or export-grade compliance certificates.
4
Learn
Post-harvest actuals feed back into the models. Accuracy improves every season — ±12% Year 1, ±7% by Year 2, per cluster.

A data moat that deepens with every season.

Every farm parcel onboarded creates labeled training data that improves model accuracy — calibrating LSTM yield models to that cluster's specific soil profiles, microclimates, and cropping patterns. A competitor entering Year 2 starts from scratch.

Founder
The data to transform Indian agriculture already exists — in satellite passes, soil sensors, and mandi ledgers. We just needed to listen.

Bhushan Darekar built Stellrix from a conviction forged at the intersection of agri-business and machine learning: that the food supply chain's biggest inefficiencies are not operational — they're informational. Before Stellrix, Bhushan worked across agri-input distribution and supply chain analytics, observing firsthand that enterprise buyers, processors, and lenders made billion-rupee decisions on instinct and last season's spreadsheets.

Stellrix is his answer to that gap — an intelligence layer that plugs into existing workflows, speaks the language of compliance and credit, and delivers prescriptive insight rather than raw data dashboards. The mission is to make Indian agriculture as data-driven as the industries it feeds.
